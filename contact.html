Good to know
Why does a song need a click?

Browsers block sound until you press something. After your first click, the jukebox can play freely.

Where is my data stored?

In your own browser. Clearing site data removes students, loans and messages.

What are the library rules?

Enrolled students may hold three books for seven days, renew once, and pay PHP 5 for each late day.

Can I change the colors?

Yes. Use the theme swatches at the top, press Random, or switch on Auto to cycle every eight seconds.
