ABOUT PIXEL CAMPUS

A small retro-style campus in your browser: music to listen to, students to manage, and books to borrow.

The website

Pixel Campus is a multi-page website made to show proper use of HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It looks like an old video game, but every part of it works.

The site has four working areas. The Music Hall plays chiptune songs, the Student Registry keeps student records, the Library lends books you can read on screen, and the Help Desk takes your messages.

Pick any of six color themes at the top of every page. The gradient background moves on its own, and the pixel font is used everywhere.

How to use the website

1. Music

Press the play button or Random song. Click a song in the list to play it. Click it again to pause. Turn on Shuffle to let the jukebox pick the next song.

2. Students

Fill in the form and press Create student. In the table, use View, Edit or Delete on any row. Type in the search box to find someone by name, ID, email or course.

3. Library

Choose a student at the Borrower desk, then press Borrow on a book. Open My loans to Read, Renew or Return it. Late books cost PHP 5 per day.

4. Themes and Contact

Click a color swatch, press Random, or switch on Auto to change themes. Use the Help Desk page to write a message. Press Esc to close any pop-up window.

How the three languages are used

HTML

Gives every page its structure: headings, forms, tables, lists and buttons with proper labels.

CSS

Draws the pixel windows, the six themes, the animated gradient and the layout that fits phones and desktops.

JavaScript

Plays the music, saves students and loans in your browser, checks forms, opens pop-ups and changes themes.
